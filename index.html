Why do atoms form ions?

Atoms are most stable with a full outer shell, like the noble gases. Metals have only 1, 2 or 3 outer electrons, so it is easiest for them to lose these and form positive ions. Non-metals have 5, 6 or 7 outer electrons, so it is easiest to gain 3, 2 or 1 and form negative ions. The group number tells you how many outer electrons an atom has.
